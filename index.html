Foundations
"Shifting from statistical mimicry to active reasoning."

Agentic Linguistics reimagines the relationship between large language models and low-resource or endangered languages.

By moving away from data-intensive training, we utilize Sense–Think–Act loops. This allows agents to consult structural grammar blueprints and lexicons in real-time, making even the most underdocumented languages fully executable in the digital age.
